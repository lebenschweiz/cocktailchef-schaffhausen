Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains which personal data we process when you visit our website or contact us. It is governed by the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

Controller

Erika’s Beauty Loft, owner Erika Hartung

Contacting us

When you contact us via WhatsApp, email, phone or the contact form, we process your details (e.g. name, phone number, email address, desired treatment and appointment) solely to answer your enquiry and schedule your appointment. Messages from the contact form are forwarded to our mailbox via the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co). Data may also be transferred to countries outside Switzerland, e.g. the USA. Please do not send health information via the form. No form data is stored on the website itself.

Our email is managed by Infomaniak Network SA in Switzerland. Emails sent to us are stored on servers in Switzerland.

Health information

Safe laser hair removal may require information about your health (e.g. contraindications). We use this sensitive data only for your treatment, keep it confidential and do not pass it on to third parties.

WhatsApp

Appointments are booked via WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd., part of Meta). When you write to us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's privacy policy also applies. Data may be transferred to countries outside Switzerland, e.g. the USA.

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit the website, technically necessary data such as IP address, date and time, page visited and browser type are processed to display the website securely and reliably.

Google Fonts and Google Maps

We use Google Fonts to display fonts, and the contact page includes a Google Maps map (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC, USA). Your IP address is transmitted to Google in the process. For more information, see Google's privacy policy.

Links to Instagram and Google reviews

Our website contains links to Instagram and to our Google profile. Data is only transmitted to the respective provider when you click such a link.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use cookies for advertising or analytics and do not use any tracking tools.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by law.

Your rights

You can request information about the data we hold about you at any time and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Simply write to us at the address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
